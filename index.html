Privacy Policy

Spin to Dine · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Spin to Dine ("the App") is operated by Michael Pisanchik. This policy explains what information the App uses and how.

Location

The App asks for your device's location to find restaurants near you. Your location is sent directly to Google's Places API to perform this search. The App does not have its own servers and does not store your location anywhere beyond your device.

No Accounts, No Personal Information

The App does not require sign-up or an account, and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.

No Advertising or Tracking

The App does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking software, and does not share any data with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google's Places API to show restaurant information (names, ratings, reviews, photos, hours, and directions). This data is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

On-Device Storage

To reduce repeated requests, the App temporarily caches restaurant data on your device. This cache is deleted automatically after a short time, or immediately if you delete the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact
